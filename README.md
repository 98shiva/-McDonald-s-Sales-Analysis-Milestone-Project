# 🍔 McDonald's Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes McDonald's sales data to uncover revenue trends, customer ordering behavior, product performance, and operational insights. Using Microsoft Excel, the dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

🎯 **Objective:** To help stakeholders understand sales performance, identify top-selling products, monitor customer demand patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Restaurant businesses generate thousands of transactions daily. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

✅ Peak ordering hours
✅ Best-selling products
✅ Revenue-driving categories
✅ Weekly and monthly sales trends
✅ Customer purchasing behavior
✅ Operational improvement opportunities

This dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics to support strategic and operational decisions.

## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

### 💰 Sales Performance Analysis

* Generated **$61K+ revenue** across **5,370 customer orders**.
* Weekly sales remained relatively stable, with peak performance observed on **Monday and Friday**.

### 👥 Customer Ordering Behavior

* Highest order volumes occurred during **lunch and evening hours**.
* **Weekdays contributed over 70% of total sales**, outperforming weekend sales.

### 🍔 Product Performance

* Burgers generated the highest overall sales contribution.
* Top-selling menu items significantly outperformed other products in revenue generation.

### 📈 Trend Analysis

* Monthly order volume showed fluctuations, highlighting changing demand patterns.
* Hourly analysis identified peak demand windows useful for staffing and inventory planning.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

🚀 Increase staffing during peak ordering hours.

📦 Optimize inventory planning based on demand patterns.

🎯 Focus marketing campaigns on top-performing products.

💰 Implement upselling strategies for high-demand menu categories.

📊 Continuously monitor weekday demand trends to maximize revenue opportunities.
